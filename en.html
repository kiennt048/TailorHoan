About us

Care in every stitch

Quynh Chau specialises in made-to-measure medical uniforms — doctors' lab coats, nursing scrubs, theatre wear, technician and pharmacist uniforms.

Beyond healthcare we also make uniforms for nurseries, schools, beauty salons, hotels and restaurants. Every order is looked after from fabric selection and pattern-making through cutting, sewing and finishing.

Fabrics chosen for healthcare

We advise on the right fabric for each role and for how often your team launders it.

Cut to real measurements

We measure each person, or work to your own size chart — not off-the-shelf bulk patterns.

Logo embroidery on request

Your logo, hospital name, department or individual names embroidered onto the garment.

Process

Four steps to a uniform that fits

  1. Consultation & quote

    We discuss style, fabric and quantity, then send a detailed quote free of charge.

  2. Design & sample approval

    We make a sample to your brief. You comment and approve before any fabric is cut.

  3. Measuring & production

    We measure each person or use your size chart, then cut, sew and check every garment.

  4. Delivery & size exchange

    Delivered to your door. We alter or exchange sizes if anything does not fit.
