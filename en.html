About us

Dedication in
every single stitch

Tailor Hoan specializes in medical uniforms — from doctor lab coats, nurse scrubs, caregiver uniforms, and surgical assistant wear to lab technician and medical staff clothing. We deeply understand the healthcare industry and its strict quality requirements.

Beyond healthcare, we also produce uniforms for kindergartens, schools, beauty salons, hotels, and restaurants. Every product is meticulously crafted from fabric selection and design to final finishing — ensuring a perfect fit, durability, and all-day comfort.

Medical-grade fabrics

Antibacterial, wrinkle-resistant, breathable fabrics that withstand industrial washing and high-temperature sterilization

Custom tailoring

Every uniform is individually measured and tailored for a perfect fit

Free logo embroidery

We embroider your hospital name, school logo, or brand directly onto the uniforms
