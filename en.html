Fabrics

We choose the fabric with you, before cutting

Fabric decides how long a uniform lasts. This is how we choose it with you.

By the role

A clinic lab coat, scrubs worn through a long shift and operating-theatre wear have different priorities for breathability, durability and freedom of movement. We advise per role rather than putting everyone in the same cloth.

By how you launder

A hospital running frequent industrial washes is not the same as a clinic laundering normally. Tell us your laundry process and we will match the fabric and the colour to it.

Specifications written into the quote

Fabric name, composition, weight and colour code are written into your quote. You can ask to see the actual fabric swatch before we cut.

Order process

4 transparent steps to custom-fitted uniforms

Strict timeline adherence, from design sampling to post-delivery sizing exchanges.

  1. Consultation & quote

    We discuss style, fabric and quantity, then send a detailed quote free of charge.

  2. Design & sample approval

    We make a sample to your brief. You comment and approve before any fabric is cut.

  3. Measuring & production

    We measure each person or use your size chart, then cut, sew and check every garment.

  4. Delivery & size exchange

    Delivered to your door. We alter or exchange sizes if anything does not fit.
